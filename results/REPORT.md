## E3 tune: label-free parameter selection

For every hypergraph and method, a setting is discarded if it yields fewer than two communities or if the largest community is more than twice the second largest (the giant-component rule). Among the remaining settings the one with the highest concentration (sum over communities of size squared, divided by n squared) is chosen, with ties going to the stricter parameter. The columns ending in coverage give the earlier rule, which picks the highest coverage among the same survivors. If nothing survives, the paper default is used. The oracle here is the best setting for each individual hypergraph (unlike E2, where parameters are fixed per cell), so the gap oracle minus chosen is always non-negative. chosen is the concentration rule and chosen_coverage the coverage rule. Hit rate is the fraction of hypergraphs where the chosen setting reaches the oracle ECS.
